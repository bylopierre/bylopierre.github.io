Housemarks support

Housemarks keeps the homes you find online in one place, so you and your partner can save, compare and discuss them.

Contact

Questions, problems or feedback: hi@lepierre.com. We usually reply within a few working days.

Common questions

How do I save a listing? Paste the listing's link in the app, or tap the Share button on any property website and choose Housemarks.

Why are some details missing? Housemarks reads the details the property website publishes. Some websites don't share a photo, price or location; you can still open the original listing.

How do I share with my partner? Open your profile, invite your partner by email, and once they accept you both see the same saved listings and comments.

How do I delete my account? Open the app and go to Settings → Delete account. You can also email hi@lepierre.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete your account and data.
